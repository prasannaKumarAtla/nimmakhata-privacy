Nimma Khata (నిమ్మ ఖాతా) - Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy (English)

App: Nimma Khata, package com.nimmakhata. Last updated: 23 November 2026.

Nimma Khata is an offline ledger for small lemon farmers. Your privacy is simple: all your data stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no server.

  • No internet. The app does not ask for the internet permission and cannot send your data anywhere on its own.
  • Your records. Sale slips, khata, labour, expenses, pesticide and fertilizer purchases are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Camera and gallery. Used only to take or choose a photo of a sale slip. The photo is stored privately inside the app. The app does not read any other photos.
  • Voice input. When you tap the microphone on a name or note field, the app asks your phone's speech recognition service (provided by Google) to listen. That service may process your audio according to Google's own privacy policy. Nimma Khata does not record or keep your voice, and only receives the text.
  • Backups. The app writes backup files only to a folder that you choose on your phone. If you copy that folder to a pendrive or SD card, that is your own choice. Nothing is uploaded.
  • No ads, no analytics, no sharing. We do not show ads, do not use analytics or tracking, do not share your data with anyone, and do not sell it.
  • Children. The app is meant for adult farmers and is not designed for children.
  • Deleting your data. Uninstalling the app deletes its private data. Backup files in the folder you chose stay there until you delete them yourself.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this page.
